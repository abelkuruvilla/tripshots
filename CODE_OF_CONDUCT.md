# Code of Conduct

TripShots follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful; criticise ideas, not people; no harassment, insults, or sharing other
people's private information (including their photos or footage) without consent.

**Reporting.** Contact the maintainers privately through the repository's private reporting channel (on GitHub:
*Security → Report a vulnerability* works for conduct reports too), or by email to a maintainer listed in
`pyproject.toml`. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and
temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they judge inappropriate, following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
